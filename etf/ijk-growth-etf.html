IJK FAQ

What is the IJK ETF?

IJK is the iShares S&P Mid-Cap 400 Growth ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $10.09B in assets.

What is IJK's total return?

Since its first trading day in Jul 2000, IJK has returned +667% with dividends reinvested, about 8.0% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned +8.0%.

Has IJK beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year IJK returned +8.0% versus +5.2% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 3.7% a year versus 9.5% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in IJK be worth today?

$10,000 invested in IJK at launch (Jul 2000) would be worth about $76,692 today with dividends reinvested, versus $77,288 in SPY over the same period.

What was IJK's worst drawdown?

IJK's largest drop from a high was -54.5%, from Jun 5, 2008 to Nov 20, 2008. It got back to its old high by Nov 4, 2010.

Does IJK pay a dividend?

Yes, small. IJK paid $0.637 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.64% yield, paid quarterly. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
