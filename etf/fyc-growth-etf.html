FYC FAQ

What is the FYC ETF?

FYC is the First Trust Small Cap Growth AlphaDEX Fund. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $1.30B in assets.

What is FYC's total return?

Since its first trading day in Apr 2011, FYC has returned +450% with dividends reinvested, about 11.5% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned +20.8%.

Has FYC beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year FYC returned +20.8% versus +10.2% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 7.2% a year versus 10.4% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in FYC be worth today?

$10,000 invested in FYC at launch (Apr 2011) would be worth about $54,950 today with dividends reinvested, versus $70,804 in SPY over the same period.

What was FYC's worst drawdown?

FYC's largest drop from a high was -47.8%, from Aug 31, 2018 to Mar 18, 2020. It got back to its old high by Nov 16, 2020.

Does FYC pay a dividend?

Yes, small. FYC paid $0.13 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.12% yield, paid annual. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
