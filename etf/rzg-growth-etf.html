RZG FAQ

What is the RZG ETF?

RZG is the Invesco S&P Smallcap 600 Pure Growth ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $106.79M in assets.

What is RZG's total return?

Since its first trading day in Mar 2006, RZG has returned +422% with dividends reinvested, about 8.3% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned +11.6%.

Has RZG beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year RZG returned +11.6% versus +8.3% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 1.9% a year versus 10.3% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in RZG be worth today?

$10,000 invested in RZG at launch (Mar 2006) would be worth about $52,156 today with dividends reinvested, versus $81,763 in SPY over the same period.

What was RZG's worst drawdown?

RZG's largest drop from a high was -58.5%, from Oct 5, 2007 to Mar 9, 2009. It got back to its old high by Nov 5, 2010.

Does RZG pay a dividend?

Yes, small. RZG paid $0.36 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.61% yield, paid quarterly. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
