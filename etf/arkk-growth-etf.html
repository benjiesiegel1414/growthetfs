ARKK FAQ

What is the ARKK ETF?

ARKK is the ARK Innovation ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $18.66B in assets.

What is ARKK's total return?

Since its first trading day in Oct 2014, ARKK has returned +277% with dividends reinvested, about 11.6% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned -5.4%.

Has ARKK beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year ARKK returned -5.4% versus +10.8% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged -8.3% a year versus 10.6% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in ARKK be worth today?

$10,000 invested in ARKK at launch (Oct 2014) would be worth about $37,713 today with dividends reinvested, versus $43,709 in SPY over the same period.

What was ARKK's worst drawdown?

ARKK's largest drop from a high was -80.9%, from Feb 12, 2021 to Dec 28, 2022. It has not yet fully recovered that high.

Does ARKK pay a dividend?

ARKK has not paid a dividend in the last 12 months. Like most growth ETFs, its return comes almost entirely from price gains.
