IWF FAQ

What is the IWF ETF?

IWF is the iShares Russell 1000 Growth ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $113.75B in assets.

What is IWF's total return?

Since its first trading day in May 2000, IWF has returned +583% with dividends reinvested, about 7.5% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned -12.0%.

Has IWF beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year IWF returned -12.0% versus +5.2% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 6.3% a year versus 9.5% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in IWF be worth today?

$10,000 invested in IWF at launch (May 2000) would be worth about $68,272 today with dividends reinvested, versus $79,768 in SPY over the same period.

What was IWF's worst drawdown?

IWF's largest drop from a high was -64.2%, from Jul 17, 2000 to Mar 9, 2009. It got back to its old high by Sep 18, 2013.

Does IWF pay a dividend?

Yes, small. IWF paid $0.432 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.42% yield, paid quarterly. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
