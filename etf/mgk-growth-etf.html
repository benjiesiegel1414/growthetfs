MGK FAQ

What is the MGK ETF?

MGK is the Vanguard Mega Cap Growth ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $31.57B in assets.

What is MGK's total return?

Since its first trading day in Dec 2007, MGK has returned +821% with dividends reinvested, about 12.5% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned -4.3%.

Has MGK beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year MGK returned -4.3% versus +5.2% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 8.3% a year versus 9.5% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in MGK be worth today?

$10,000 invested in MGK at launch (Dec 2007) would be worth about $92,098 today with dividends reinvested, versus $65,746 in SPY over the same period.

What was MGK's worst drawdown?

MGK's largest drop from a high was -48.0%, from Dec 27, 2007 to Mar 9, 2009. It got back to its old high by Jan 14, 2011.

Does MGK pay a dividend?

Yes, small. MGK paid $0.3038 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.40% yield, paid quarterly. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
