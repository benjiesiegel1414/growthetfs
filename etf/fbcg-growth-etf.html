FBCG FAQ

What is the FBCG ETF?

FBCG is the Fidelity Blue Chip Growth ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $6.99B in assets.

What is FBCG's total return?

Since its first trading day in Jun 2020, FBCG has returned +150% with dividends reinvested, about 15.3% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned -3.3%.

Has FBCG beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year FBCG returned -3.3% versus +5.2% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 7.1% a year versus 9.5% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in FBCG be worth today?

Does FBCG pay a dividend?

Yes, small. FBCG paid $0.02 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.04% yield, paid annual. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
