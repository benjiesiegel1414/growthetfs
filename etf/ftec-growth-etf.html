FTEC FAQ

What is the FTEC ETF?

FTEC is the Fidelity MSCI Information Technology Index. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $19.18B in assets.

What is FTEC's total return?

Since its first trading day in Oct 2013, FTEC has returned +948% with dividends reinvested, about 19.7% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned +6.6%.

Has FTEC beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year FTEC returned +6.6% versus +5.2% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 12.3% a year versus 9.5% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in FTEC be worth today?

$10,000 invested in FTEC at launch (Oct 2013) would be worth about $104,756 today with dividends reinvested, versus $48,997 in SPY over the same period.

What was FTEC's worst drawdown?

FTEC's largest drop from a high was -35.0%, from Dec 27, 2021 to Oct 14, 2022. It got back to its old high by Nov 20, 2023.

Does FTEC pay a dividend?

Yes, small. FTEC paid $1.109 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.48% yield, paid quarterly. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
