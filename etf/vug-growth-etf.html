VUG FAQ

What is the VUG ETF?

VUG is the Vanguard Growth ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $208.65B in assets.

What is VUG's total return?

Since its first trading day in Jan 2004, VUG has returned +1136% with dividends reinvested, about 11.7% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned +3.7%.

Has VUG beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year VUG returned +3.7% versus +10.8% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 9.2% a year versus 10.6% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in VUG be worth today?

$10,000 invested in VUG at launch (Jan 2004) would be worth about $123,563 today with dividends reinvested, versus $96,373 in SPY over the same period.

What was VUG's worst drawdown?

VUG's largest drop from a high was -50.7%, from Oct 31, 2007 to Mar 9, 2009. It got back to its old high by Feb 8, 2011.

Does VUG pay a dividend?

Yes, small. VUG paid $0.3458 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.43% yield, paid quarterly. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
