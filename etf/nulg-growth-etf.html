NULG FAQ

What is the NULG ETF?

NULG is the Nuveen ESG Large-Cap Growth ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $2.68B in assets.

What is NULG's total return?

Since its first trading day in Dec 2016, NULG has returned +360% with dividends reinvested, about 16.6% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned +6.1%.

Has NULG beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year NULG returned +6.1% versus +10.8% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 7.9% a year versus 10.6% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in NULG be worth today?

$10,000 invested in NULG at launch (Dec 2016) would be worth about $46,005 today with dividends reinvested, versus $37,307 in SPY over the same period.

What was NULG's worst drawdown?

Does NULG pay a dividend?

Yes, small. NULG paid $0.111 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.11% yield, paid annual. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
