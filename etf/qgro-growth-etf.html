QGRO FAQ

What is the QGRO ETF?

QGRO is the American Century U.S. Quality Growth ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $1.64B in assets.

What is QGRO's total return?

Since its first trading day in Sep 2018, QGRO has returned +175% with dividends reinvested, about 13.1% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned -3.5%.

Has QGRO beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year QGRO returned -3.5% versus +8.3% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 7.2% a year versus 10.3% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in QGRO be worth today?

$10,000 invested in QGRO at launch (Sep 2018) would be worth about $27,509 today with dividends reinvested, versus $28,068 in SPY over the same period.

What was QGRO's worst drawdown?

QGRO's largest drop from a high was -32.6%, from Feb 19, 2020 to Mar 23, 2020. It got back to its old high by Jun 2, 2020.

Does QGRO pay a dividend?

Yes, small. QGRO paid $0.235 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.22% yield, paid quarterly. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
