SFY FAQ

What is the SFY ETF?

SFY is the SoFi Select 500 ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $607.74M in assets.

What is SFY's total return?

Since its first trading day in Apr 2019, SFY has returned +195% with dividends reinvested, about 15.2% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned +4.9%.

Has SFY beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year SFY returned +4.9% versus +8.2% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 10.3% a year versus 10.3% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in SFY be worth today?

$10,000 invested in SFY at launch (Apr 2019) would be worth about $29,482 today with dividends reinvested, versus $27,717 in SPY over the same period.

What was SFY's worst drawdown?

SFY's largest drop from a high was -33.2%, from Feb 19, 2020 to Mar 23, 2020. It got back to its old high by Aug 3, 2020.

Does SFY pay a dividend?

Yes, small. SFY paid $1.245 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.92% yield, paid semiannual. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
