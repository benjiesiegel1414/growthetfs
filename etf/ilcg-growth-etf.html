ILCG FAQ

What is the ILCG ETF?

ILCG is the iShares Morningstar Growth ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $2.97B in assets.

What is ILCG's total return?

Since its first trading day in Jul 2004, ILCG has returned +946% with dividends reinvested, about 11.1% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned +2.4%.

Has ILCG beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year ILCG returned +2.4% versus +10.7% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 7.8% a year versus 10.5% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in ILCG be worth today?

$10,000 invested in ILCG at launch (Jul 2004) would be worth about $104,599 today with dividends reinvested, versus $97,183 in SPY over the same period.

What was ILCG's worst drawdown?

ILCG's largest drop from a high was -53.0%, from Oct 31, 2007 to Nov 20, 2008. It got back to its old high by Feb 16, 2012.

Does ILCG pay a dividend?

Yes, small. ILCG paid $0.479 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.46% yield, paid quarterly. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
