IJT FAQ

What is the IJT ETF?

IJT is the iShares S&P Small-Cap 600 Growth ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $7.41B in assets.

What is IJT's total return?

Since its first trading day in Jul 2000, IJT has returned +901% with dividends reinvested, about 9.1% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned +18.8%.

Has IJT beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year IJT returned +18.8% versus +10.1% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 3.3% a year versus 10.3% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in IJT be worth today?

$10,000 invested in IJT at launch (Jul 2000) would be worth about $100,050 today with dividends reinvested, versus $80,530 in SPY over the same period.

What was IJT's worst drawdown?

IJT's largest drop from a high was -57.6%, from Oct 10, 2007 to Mar 9, 2009. It got back to its old high by Dec 21, 2010.

Does IJT pay a dividend?

Yes, small. IJT paid $1.258 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.79% yield, paid quarterly. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
