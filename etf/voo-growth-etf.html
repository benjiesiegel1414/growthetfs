VOO FAQ

What is the VOO ETF?

VOO is the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $1,019.04B in assets.

What is VOO's total return?

Since its first trading day in Sep 2010, VOO has returned +772% with dividends reinvested, about 14.3% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned +10.8%.

Has VOO beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year VOO returned +10.8% versus +10.8% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 10.6% a year versus 10.6% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in VOO be worth today?

$10,000 invested in VOO at launch (Sep 2010) would be worth about $87,222 today with dividends reinvested, versus $86,700 in SPY over the same period.

What was VOO's worst drawdown?

VOO's largest drop from a high was -34.0%, from Feb 19, 2020 to Mar 23, 2020. It got back to its old high by Aug 10, 2020.

Does VOO pay a dividend?

Yes, small. VOO paid $7.428 per share over the last 12 months, about a 1.12% yield, paid quarterly. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
