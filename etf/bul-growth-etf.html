BUL FAQ

What is the BUL ETF?

BUL is the Pacer US Cash Cows Growth ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $134.30M in assets.

What is BUL's total return?

Since its first trading day in May 2019, BUL has returned +153% with dividends reinvested, about 13.1% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned +15.8%.

Has BUL beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year BUL returned +15.8% versus +10.7% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 7.3% a year versus 10.5% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in BUL be worth today?

$10,000 invested in BUL at launch (May 2019) would be worth about $25,280 today with dividends reinvested, versus $27,552 in SPY over the same period.

What was BUL's worst drawdown?

BUL's largest drop from a high was -37.1%, from Jan 17, 2020 to Mar 23, 2020. It got back to its old high by Jun 5, 2020.

Does BUL pay a dividend?

Yes, small. BUL paid $0.097 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.16% yield, paid semiannual. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
