IMCG FAQ

What is the IMCG ETF?

IMCG is the iShares Morningstar Mid-Cap Growth ETF. It is one of 105 growth ETFs tracked on GrowthETFs.com, with about $4.04B in assets.

What is IMCG's total return?

Since its first trading day in Jul 2004, IMCG has returned +942% with dividends reinvested, about 11.1% a year. Over the last 12 months it returned +14.2%.

Has IMCG beaten the S&P 500?

Over the last year IMCG returned +14.2% versus +10.1% for SPY. Over 5 years it averaged 4.2% a year versus 10.3% for SPY.

How much would $10,000 in IMCG be worth today?

$10,000 invested in IMCG at launch (Jul 2004) would be worth about $104,231 today with dividends reinvested, versus $96,596 in SPY over the same period.

What was IMCG's worst drawdown?

IMCG's largest drop from a high was -59.0%, from Oct 31, 2007 to Nov 20, 2008. It got back to its old high by Feb 11, 2011.

Does IMCG pay a dividend?

Yes, small. IMCG paid $0.62 per share over the last 12 months, about a 0.70% yield, paid quarterly. Growth ETFs reinvest most of their return into price gains rather than paying it out.
